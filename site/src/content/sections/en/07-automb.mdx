autoMBIST is the closest open-source project: an MBIST, BIRA and BISR generator for OpenRAM macros on
sky130. Its exact 2-D solver runs on the tester; the analyzers it puts on the chip are single-pass and
two-pass heuristics, which its authors document as approximations. It ships a Python model of those
analyzers, and that model is run here unchanged on the fault maps of the previous section.

<DataTable id="automb" />

Two readings of its spare budget are shown. In the word reading a spare row replaces one word and a spare
column one bit lane, the same resources TRIAD has without ECC. In the physical reading, autoMBIST's native
one for a column-muxed macro, a spare row replaces a physical row of two words. Its exact solver and ours
agree on every one of the 800,000 maps in the word reading. The gain over it comes from the two things it
does not have, ECC as a repair resource and per-partition steering, and both cost hardware: six check bits
per word and the steering tables. A hardware comparison on the same macro has not been made.
