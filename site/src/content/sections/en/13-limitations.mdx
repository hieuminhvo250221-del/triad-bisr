---
title: "Limitations"
kicker: "LIMITS"
order: 13
id: limitations
---
- No silicon. Every result is simulation, static analysis or layout.
- The memory is small (256 words). Repair logic that is modest for a megabit array dominates here.
- The macro's timing model has one corner and is analytical, so the reported clock mainly reflects the
  wrapper logic.
- Partitions are tested one at a time, so coupling faults between partitions and address-decoder faults
  only show in the final verification pass, which reports failure instead of re-analysing.
- Spare words are flip-flops and are assumed fault-free.
- The defect mixes are assumptions; results are reported per mix because they depend on it.
- Soft-error rates are assumed, and multi-bit upsets are not modelled.
- The ECC-first baselines were built from published descriptions, without the full papers.
