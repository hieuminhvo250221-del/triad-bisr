Every allocator receives the same 10,000 random fault maps at each defect count from 1 to 30, on the
256 × 30-bit array with two spare columns. Four defect mixes are used. Three follow mixes reported in
published repair-analysis studies, as collected by a literature search and not yet checked against the
original papers; the fourth is our own.

<DataTable id="n90" />

Partitioned steering helps most when isolated cells and small clusters dominate, and not at all when most
defects are whole lines, where the number of spare words is the limit. An ECC budget raises the tolerance
sharply for cell defects. With K = 0 and no partitions the allocator coincides with exact 2-D repair, as
it must. The spares are assumed fault-free in this experiment.
