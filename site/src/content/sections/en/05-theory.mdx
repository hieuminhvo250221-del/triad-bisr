The allocation problem is stated on a bipartite graph of words and bit columns. Choose at most R words
and at most S columns so that every remaining word keeps at most one faulty bit, and at most K words keep
exactly one. With K = 0 this is the classical spare-allocation problem.

Each statement is exercised by tests against exhaustive search on small instances. The proofs have not
yet been reviewed by a second person, and the hardness proof in particular should be read with that in
mind.
