The macro stores 32 bits per word. Bits 0 to 29 hold a Hsiao SEC-DED codeword (24 data bits and 6 check
bits); bits 30 and 31 are the two spare columns. Spare words are flip-flop registers in the wrapper,
selected by address comparison. Nothing inside the macro is changed.

<ArchDiagram />

A write encodes the data, copies the bits named by the steering table of the word's partition into the
spare columns, and sends the 32 bits to the macro. A read undoes the steering, substitutes a spare word
when the address matches, and decodes. The macro launches its output on the falling clock edge, so
steering, spare-word selection and ECC decoding share the remaining half cycle before the output register.

Because sky130 has no eFuse, the repair is soft. A serial port shifts the repair state out after a test
and back in after reset, so the test need not run again at every power-up.
